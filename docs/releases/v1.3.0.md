# v1.3.0

## New

- Play local MP3 files from `/sdcard/Music` in the Music app, with Play/Pause,
  Previous/Next, playlist selection, progress, auto-next and Repeat.
- Music owns the audio resources while open: Xiaozhi capture, AFE/wakeword and
  decoder are stopped while its transport connection is retained. Quick Settings
  over Music preserves playback. Leaving Music stops playback; returning does
  not automatically restart it and Xiaozhi returns to wakeword waiting.
- Unified terminal System Stats diagnostics, disabled in the production build.

## Fixes

- Preserve an app-exit Stop request when the command queue temporarily refuses it.
- Coordinate Music session closure and decoder recovery without changing the
  existing Suspend/Resume behavior.
- Reduce internal SRAM usage through Wi-Fi TX buffer and secondary worker stack
  sizing, external Music worker stacks, and the MEMORY resampler profile.

## Known limitations

- Home Xiaozhi CPU pressure and occasional self-echo remain under investigation;
  this release does not claim an AEC or Home CPU fix.
- Playback progress is elapsed wall time while Playing, not a decoder-reported
  playback position. Seeking is not provided.

## Installation

Use the ESP-VoCat v1.0 package for ESP32-S3 with 16 MB Flash. It includes an
app-only image, a merged factory image, individual partition images, flashing
instructions and SHA-256 checksums. For the same board and partition layout,
flash the app-only image at `0xb0000` to retain settings and stored data.
Factory flashing overwrites settings and data; back up anything needed first.

LCD sleep does not put the ESP32 into light/deep sleep. Third-party licensing
and asset provenance are documented in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`; component
license texts are included in the firmware package.
